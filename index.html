ABOUT OUR PROJECT

Smart AI Solution for Resume Analysis & Interview Preparation

ResumeAI Pro is an AI-powered web application designed to help students, fresh graduates, and job seekers improve their resumes and prepare confidently for job interviews.

Artificial Intelligence

AI analyzes your resume, estimates ATS score, identifies missing skills, and provides smart suggestions.

Why ResumeAI Pro?

Many students create resumes without knowing whether they are ATS-friendly or suitable for the desired job role.

ResumeAI Pro solves this problem using Artificial Intelligence. It evaluates resumes, identifies missing skills, provides personalized improvement suggestions, and helps users practice interviews through AI-powered mock interview sessions.
